Our story

A Rajkot maker of plumbing pipes and fittings

Shreenath Polytech was established in 2017 in Rajkot, Gujarat, as a sole proprietorship led by Dhaval Patel.

Under the registered CAPSON® brand, the company makes UPVC and CPVC pipe and fitting for hot and cold water plumbing, along with agri and garden pipes. We run with a compact team of up to 10 people from Shapar, Kotda Sangani, near Rajkot.

Our catalogue covers 62 products across three lines, from SCH-40 pipes and brass-insert fittings to ball valves, clamps, solvent cement and level pipe.
