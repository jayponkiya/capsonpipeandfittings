Use CPVC (SDR-11 or SDR-13.5) for lines that carry hot water. UPVC is meant for cold water lines. If unsure, ask your plumber or message us with the job details.

Both describe pipe wall thickness. SCH (schedule) is a wall-thickness class: SCH-80 has a thicker wall than SCH-40. SDR (standard dimension ratio) is the outside diameter divided by wall thickness, so a lower SDR (11) means a thicker wall than a higher one (13.5).

Use the solvent made for the material: UPVC solvent cement for UPVC, CPVC solvent cement for CPVC. Both are listed in the catalogue in 50, 118 and 250 ml tins.
