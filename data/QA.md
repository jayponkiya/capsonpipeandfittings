## Display-text changes (numbers untouched)

- Typos fixed: PACKINGIN BOX → Packing in box, THRED → Thread, SOKET → Socket, FOR TEE → Four Way Tee (Cross Tee), TRANSACTION BUSH → Transition Bush, LEVAL → Level, METALIC → Metallic, TRANSPERENT → Transparent, BAND → Bend, RED. ELBOW → Reducing Elbow, 45* → 45°.
- Size notation normalised: `1.1/4"` and `1 1/4"` → `1-1/4"`; `2'` (pipe tables) → `2"`; `3/4X1/2"`, `1*3/4"` → `3/4" × 1/2"`, `1" × 3/4"`.
- PP Long Plug variants: `BLK SOLID` → Black solid, `W.SOLID` → White solid.
- CPVC SDR-11 pipe uses 5 m (not 6 m) as printed, so its keys are `weight5m` / `pkg5m`.
- Catalogue "RATE" is the dealer rate in ₹, GST extra (stored as `rate`, hidden on the site while `SHOW_PRICES` is false).
