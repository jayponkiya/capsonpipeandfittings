01 · Colour

Colour

From the logo and brochure. Contrast ratios are measured live against white and black text (AA needs 4.5:1 for body text).

  • Capson Redvar(--red) Brand red: swooshes, accents, red on black
  • Red (UI)var(--red-ui) Buttons and small red text on light backgrounds
  • Red Darkvar(--red-dark) Hover states, deep swoosh band
  • Blackvar(--black) Hero, header, footer, dark sections
  • Inkvar(--ink) Body text, dark cards
  • Whitevar(--white) Page background, text on dark
  • Soft Greyvar(--bg-soft) Alternate section background
  • CPVC Creamvar(--cpvc-cream) CPVC line accent
  • Water Bluevar(--water-blue) Agri accent, focus ring, flow line
  • Mutedvar(--muted) Secondary text on light
  • Muted on darkvar(--muted-on-dark) Secondary text on black
